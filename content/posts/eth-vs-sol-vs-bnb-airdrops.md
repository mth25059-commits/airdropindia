The "which chain wins?" debate isn't very interesting. The "which chain has paid out the most airdrops?" debate, on the other hand, has a clear leader.

| Chain | Notable drops | Avg payout per active user |

Solana ate everyone's lunch in 2024. Whether that continues depends on adoption.

## Where to focus in 2025

### 1. Solana — still the highest yield per hour

The Solana ecosystem is back. Restaking on Jito, MEV on Jupiter, social on platforms like Drip & Backpack. Low gas (~₹1 per tx) makes farming feasible from India.

**Get started:** Buy SOL on CoinDCX, withdraw to Phantom, stake JitoSOL, swap on Jupiter.

### 2. Base — Coinbase's L2

Coinbase has not confirmed a token but the points programs in DeFi protocols on Base (Aerodrome, Friend.tech etc.) and the sheer volume of activity strongly hint at a future drop.

**Get started:** Bridge ETH to Base via the official bridge. Swap on Aerodrome. Mint Zora NFTs.

### 3. EigenLayer + Restaking AVS

EigenLayer has its own EIGEN token, but the AVSs (services that consume EigenLayer security) are each running their own points programs. This is a multi-airdrop play.

**Get started:** Buy stETH, deposit to EigenLayer, delegate to operators. Each AVS you opt into gives separate points.

### 4. zkSync follow-up + Linea

Both chains have hinted at future programs. Linea has explicit XP. zkSync's first drop disappointed many — a follow-up is likely.

**Get started:** Use these chains actively, mint NFTs, swap, lend. Don't single-day farm — sybil filters punish that.

### 5. BNB Megadrop

Lower upside per drop, but Binance hosts them frequently. Lock BNB, complete tasks, claim tokens.

**Get started:** Hold BNB on Binance, complete Web3 wallet tasks.

These are post-Indian-tax estimates assuming you sell when received. After 30% + cess + 1% TDS, you keep about **68%** of the gross.

## Indian-friendly mechanics

Some practical points for Indian users:

- **Funding wallets:** CoinDCX → withdraw to chain → use. CoinDCX charges flat ₹15–50 per withdrawal usually. Plan trips.
- **TDS overhead:** Every sell of every airdropped token triggers 1% TDS. Plan to consolidate.
- **Gas:** Solana ~₹1, BNB ~₹3, Ethereum L2s ~₹20-50. Ethereum L1 mainnet is unreasonable for most farming.

## Don't sleep on the boring chains

Polygon zkEVM, Arbitrum (STIP), and Optimism (RetroPGF) keep distributing rewards quietly. Yields per hour are higher than chasing hype.

## TL;DR

Pick **one or two ecosystems** and go deep. Spreading across 8 chains thin is sybil-flagged. Solana + 1 Ethereum L2 (Base or Linea) is the most efficient combo for Indian users right now.
