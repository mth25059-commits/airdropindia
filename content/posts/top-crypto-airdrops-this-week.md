## 1. Linea Points Program (Ethereum L2)

ConsenSys's zkEVM L2 has been quietly rewarding active users with Voyage XP. A token launch is widely expected.

- **What to do:** Bridge a small amount of ETH to Linea, swap on Lynex/Echodex, mint a couple of NFTs, lend on Mendi.
- **Time required:** 30–45 min initially, plus weekly check-ins.
- **Estimated value:** $300–800 if you stay active.

## 2. Jito Restaking (Solana)

Liquid restaking on Solana. Hold JitoSOL, earn points.

- **What to do:** Buy SOL on a local exchange, withdraw to Phantom, stake to JitoSOL via the official site.
- **Tax note:** Selling your eventual JTO reward will trigger 30% + 1% TDS like any other token sale.

## Indian tax reminder

Every airdrop has two tax events:
1. **On receipt** of the token at fair value (income, slab rate).
2. **On sale** — 30% flat + 4% cess + 1% TDS if total sells > ₹10k in FY.

Use our [tax calculator →](/tax) to estimate before you sell.

## Safety checklist

- ✅ Use a burner wallet for new airdrops
- ✅ Bookmark official URLs — never trust DMs or "support" links
- ✅ Read every transaction before signing — `set approval for all` is the most common drain
